Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you provide directly: your name, email address, and the expense data you log within the app. We do not collect payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — storing your expenses and displaying them back to you. We do not use your data for advertising or share it with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secure database. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Expense records are tied to your account and not accessible by other users.

4. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies to keep you logged in. These cookies are essential for the service to function and are not used for tracking or advertising.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. We will process such requests within 30 days.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a notice within the app.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, reach us at privacy@spendly.app.
